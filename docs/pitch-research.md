## Ready-to-Use Pitch Inserts

### 15-second opener

"WHO now estimates at least one in ten medicines in low- and middle-income countries are substandard or falsified, and the annual burden is about US$30.5 billion. Meridian is built to make that investigation workload tractable in minutes."

### 15-second semaglutide bridge

"WHO issued a semaglutide alert in June 2024, and FDA later documented counterfeit Ozempic entering legitimate U.S. supply chains in 2025. This is exactly the operational gap Meridian addresses."

### 10-second logistics line

"OECD estimates US$467 billion in counterfeit trade, with about 65% of seizures moving via small parcels and mail, which is why online cross-border investigations matter."

## Judge Q&A Guardrails

Use:
- "WHO estimates..."
- "FDA reported in updates dated 2023 and 2025..."
- "OECD-EUIPO estimates..."
- "INTERPOL reported..."

Avoid:
- Absolute claims like "all Ozempic sold online is fake"
- Causal claims not in sources (for example, specific death counts tied directly to Ozempic counterfeits)
- Older unsourced numbers if a newer official statistic exists
